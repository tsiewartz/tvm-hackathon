Before you arrive

We build in Snowsight Workspaces (Snowflake's browser-based editor), so there's almost nothing to install. Check these things before Friday.

Pick your teamChoose your team in the Your team… menu at the top. Every command on the site then shows your team number and schema. Saved in your browser only.

Checklist

  • Snowflake: you can log in to TVM's dev account in Snowsight and switch to your dev role
  • Data: you can query a table you might want to use. Run SELECT * FROM <table> LIMIT 10; in a worksheet
  • Dev access: you can log in to TVM's Snowflake account with your dev role. At 09:00 you run a 2-minute preflight check; no GitHub needed
  • CoCo: open a workspace in Snowsight and check that the CoCo panel opens and answers a question, e.g. "What can you help me with?". Nothing to install
  • Claude Code: ready on one laptop per team (the agent builder's, which also runs the demo)
  • Think: which questions do people in your part of the business keep asking the data team? Bring 3–5

Claude Code (one per team)

Licence and policyCheck that your Claude plan and TVM's IT policy allow Claude Code and custom MCP servers. If they don't, the same agent also works in Snowflake CoWork at ai.snowflake.com, which every team tests in anyway.
