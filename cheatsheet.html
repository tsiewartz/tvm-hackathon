Our pattern: expose only the agent. The other tool types exist, but they let clients bypass your agent's instructions:

TypePoints at
CORTEX_AGENT_RUNA Cortex Agent
CORTEX_ANALYST_MESSAGEA semantic view (text-to-SQL)
CORTEX_SEARCH_SERVICE_QUERYA Cortex Search service
GENERICA UDF or stored procedure
SYSTEM_EXECUTE_SQLArbitrary SQL with your role (use with care)

Your build assistant: Snowflake CoCo

Every team builds with CoCo (Cortex Code), the AI assistant built into Snowsight Workspaces. Open it from the workspace next to your dbt files. It reads your project, knows Snowflake, and can write and fix the semantic view, agent and dbt models with you. It runs with your role, so it only sees what you can see.

✓ Good prompts

  • "Look at source('tvm_marts', ...) and suggest a semantic view for our 10 golden questions"
  • "Add a metric for monthly churn rate to sv_team_1.sql, with a clear description"
  • "This verified query fails with invalid identifier. Fix it"
  • "Write agent instructions so it declines questions about individual customers"

✗ Watch out

  • Accepting changes without reading them. Review every diff before you build
  • Letting it edit outside your team project
  • Using it instead of checking the numbers. The business tester still checks answers against known values
