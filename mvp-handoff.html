MVP handoff

The day ends with four MVPs, not four demos. Here's how to hand yours over so it keeps moving next week.

Before you leave

  • The team project is downloaded from the driver's workspace (right-click team_n » Download) and handed to the owner, who puts it in TVM's repo after today
  • golden_questions.md is in the project folder, with the current score (x/10)
  • NEXT_STEPS.md is filled in (template below)
  • A product owner and a tech owner are named
  • Your PAT is revoked, or left to expire
